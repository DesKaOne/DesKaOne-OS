# Community Code of Conduct

DesKaOne OS welcomes contributors regardless of background or experience.

## Our standards
Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, focus on technical evidence, credit contributors, welcome newcomers and disagree without personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory insults, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, intimidation and persistent disruptive conduct.

## Reporting
For community conduct concerns, contact project maintainers through a private GitHub channel where available. Do not publish sensitive personal details in Issues. Maintainers will review reports confidentially to the extent practical and may warn, restrict or remove participants.

This policy applies to project Issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.
